Our Fresh Heritage

From humble beginnings in 1993 as 'Fruit & Veg City' in Access Park, Cape Town, founders Brian and Mike Coppin set out to create an authentic marketplace theatre where customers could experience the best fresh food at great prices.

Today, Food Lover's Market is South Africa's leading fresh food retail brand, supporting hundreds of local farmers, bakers, and food producers nationwide.

Our Mission

To be the best fresh food retail store in South Africa by consistently providing exceptional quality, competitive value, and an unmatched theatre-of-food shopping experience.

Our Vision

To cultivate a healthier, happier community by connecting urban consumers with honest, sustainably farmed fresh produce while fostering environmental care.
